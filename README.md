# PROJECT 10 – FOOD WASTE MONITORING SYSTEM

## About the Project
The Food Waste Monitoring System is designed to monitor and analyze food consumption and wastage over multiple days. It accepts the quantity of food prepared and the quantity of food served each day. It calculates the amount of food wasted and identifies the total food waste. This system helps monitor food usage and encourages the reduction of food wastage.

## Technologies Used
- **Python** – Used to develop the food waste monitoring program.
- **Lists** – Used to store daily food quantities.
- **Loops** – Used to process daily food records.
- **Conditional Statements** – Used to identify food wastage.
- **Arithmetic Operations** – Used to calculate wasted food and percentages.
- **Strings** – Used to display daily reports.

## Main Features
- Accepts the number of days to monitor.
- Records the quantity of food prepared each day.
- Records the quantity of food served each day.
- Calculates daily food wastage.
- Calculates daily waste percentage.
- Calculates total food prepared and served.
- Identifies the day with the highest food wastage.
- Displays a complete food waste report.

## Algorithm
1. Start the program.
2. Get the number of days to monitor.
3. Enter the day name.
4. Enter the quantity of food prepared.
5. Enter the quantity of food served.
6. Calculate food wasted by subtracting served quantity from prepared quantity.
7. Calculate the waste percentage.
8. Store the daily food records.
9. Calculate total food prepared, served, and wasted.
10. Identify the day with the highest food wastage.
11. Display the food waste report.
12. Stop the program.
